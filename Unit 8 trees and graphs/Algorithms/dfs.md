## DFS Algorithm

**Algorithm:** `DFS(G, s)`  

1. Start from vertex `s`.  
2. Mark `s` as visited.  
3. Visit one unvisited adjacent vertex.  
4. Repeat steps 2–3 for that vertex.  
5. Continue until no unvisited neighbor exists.  
6. Backtrack to the previous vertex.  
7. Repeat until all vertices are visited.
