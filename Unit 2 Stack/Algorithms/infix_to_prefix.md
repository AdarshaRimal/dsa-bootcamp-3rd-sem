## INFIX TO PREFIX

### ✅ Algorithm 

**Step 1:** Reverse the infix expression  
**Step 2:** Replace `'('` with `')'` and vice versa  
**Step 3:** Convert reversed expression to postfix (replace with above infix_to_postfix algorithms in exams)  
**Step 4:** Reverse the postfix result  
**Step 5:** Final result is prefix
