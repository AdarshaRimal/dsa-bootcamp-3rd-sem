## QUICK SORT

### Algorithm

**Step 1:** Choose a pivot element from the array.  
**Step 2:** Partition the array so that elements smaller than the pivot are placed to its left and elements greater than the pivot are placed to its right.  
**Step 3:** After partitioning, the pivot is placed in its correct sorted position.  
**Step 4:** Recursively apply the same process to the left sub-array (elements before the pivot).  
**Step 5:** Recursively apply the same process to the right sub-array (elements after the pivot).  
**Step 6:** Continue this process until each sub-array contains only one element or is empty.  
**Step 7:** End.
